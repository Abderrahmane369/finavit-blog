# Vitamin C: Foods, Benefits, and What You Should Know

Vitamin C is an essential nutrient found in many fruits and vegetables. It plays several important roles in the body, including supporting normal immune function and helping the body produce collagen.

## What is vitamin C?

Vitamin C, also known as ascorbic acid, is a water-soluble vitamin. The body cannot produce it on its own, so it must come from food or other dietary sources.

## Foods rich in vitamin C

Some foods naturally contain substantial amounts of vitamin C, including:

- Oranges and other citrus fruits
- Red and green bell peppers
- Kiwi
- Strawberries
- Broccoli
- Brussels sprouts

The amount of vitamin C varies depending on the food, serving size, storage, and preparation method.

## Why does the body need vitamin C?

Vitamin C contributes to:

- Normal collagen formation.
- Normal functioning of the immune system.
- Protection of cells from oxidative stress.
- Improved absorption of iron from plant-based foods.

## How can you include more vitamin C in your diet?

Try adding fresh fruit to breakfast, including vegetables in meals, or choosing vitamin C-rich snacks such as kiwi and strawberries.

Eating a varied diet is a practical way to obtain vitamin C alongside other essential nutrients.
